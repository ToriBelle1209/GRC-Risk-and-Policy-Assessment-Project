# Organization Profile

## 1. Organization Overview

**Organization:** Caribbean Financial Services (CFS)  
**Industry:** Financial Services  
**Location:** Barbados  
**Employees:** Approximately 50  
**Organization Type:** Fictional

Caribbean Financial Services (CFS) is a small fictional organization that provides financial services to individual and business customers.

CFS relies on information technology to manage customer information, employee records, financial information, communications, and daily business operations.


## 2. Confidential Information Handled

CFS handles several types of sensitive and business-critical information, including:

- Customer personal information
- Customer financial information
- Customer account information
- Employee personal information
- Business records
- Internal company information
- Authentication credentials

Because CFS handles personal and financial information, protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of its information is important to the organization.


## 3. Technology and Systems

CFS uses a combination of cloud-based and organizational technology systems, including:

- Employee laptops
- Email
- Cloud-based productivity applications
- Customer-facing web applications
- Internal business applications
- File storage systems
- User accounts and authentication systems
- Third-party service providers


## 4. Users

CFS has approximately 50 employees who use the organization's technology systems to perform their job responsibilities.

Different employees have different levels of access depending on their roles and responsibilities.

For example:

- Customer service employees may access customer account information to assist with questions, account issues, and requests.
- Finance employees may access customer financial records to process payments, and review transactions.
- Human resources employees may need access to employee information to manage hiring, payroll, benefits, and other employment-related responsibilities..
- Managers may have additional administrative responsibilities such as approving requests, reviewing reports, or overseeing employees.
- IT personnel may have access to certain systems so they can maintain systems, troubleshoot technical problems, manage user accounts, and respond to security issues.


## 5. Third-Party Services

CFS relies on third-party service providers for some business and technology functions.

These may include:

- Cloud software providers
- Email and productivity platforms 
- Payment service providers
- IT service providers
- Data storage providers

Some CFS information may be stored, processed, or accessed through these third-party services. Because CFS does not directly control these companies' systems, using third-party services can create additional cybersecurity and privacy risks. CFS should therefore consider the security practices of third-party providers when evaluating organizational risk.

## 6. Security Concerns

Based on the organization's activities, information, and technology environment, several cybersecurity risks may affect CFS.

Potential concerns include:

- Phishing and social engineering attacks
- Unauthorized access to systems or information
- Lost or stolen company devices
- Third-party security incidents
- Improper access after an employee leaves the organization
- Malware or ransomware
- Exposure of customer information
- Weak authentication practices


## 7. Assessment Scope

For this initial assessment, the project will focus on five cybersecurity risks:

- Phishing and social engineering attacks
- Unauthorized user access
- Lost or stolen devices
- Third-party security incidents
- Former employees retaining access to organizational systems

The assessment will evaluate the likelihood and potential impact of these risks and identify appropriate risk treatment recommendations.

## 8. Project Assumptions

Because CFS is a fictional organization, this assessment uses several assumptions for educational purposes.

The assessment assumes that:

- CFS uses cloud-based applications.
- Employees access company systems only using their organizational accounts.
- Employees may access company information remotely.
- CFS stores sensitive customer and employee information.
- CFS uses third-party technology providers.
- CFS has basic cybersecurity practices but does not have a fully developed GRC program.

These assumptions are intended to provide a realistic scenario for demonstrating foundational GRC concepts.
